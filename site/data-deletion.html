How to delete your data

Healthcare Coordination Project · Last updated 23 September 2026

You can ask for everything held about you to be deleted, at any time, without giving a reason.

By message

Reply to any conversation with the word DELETE. Your request is passed to a person, who confirms it with you before anything is removed.

By email

Email tasrifahmedmohsin@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request, from the address or about the phone number the information relates to.

What gets deleted

  • Your messages and any voice notes, photographs or documents you sent.
  • Symptoms, measurements and history recorded from them.
  • Your case records and the contact details linking them to you.

What may be kept, and why

  • Records a clinician is required to retain for a legal or clinical reason. If this applies, you will be told which records and why.
  • Information that no longer identifies you and cannot be linked back to you, such as counts used to check the system is working correctly.

How long it takes

Requests are acknowledged within 7 days and completed within 30 days. You will be told when it is done.

Withdrawing consent instead

If you want processing to stop but your history kept, say so. Withdrawing consent halts further processing without deleting what already exists.

See also the privacy policy. This project is academic work and is not currently a healthcare service.
