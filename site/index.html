Healthcare Coordination Project

Academic project · Bangladesh

This is not a healthcare service. It is student research software. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, it is not open to the public, and it currently runs on synthetic test data. If you have a medical concern, contact a registered doctor or your nearest health facility.

A care-coordination layer that organises what a patient describes into a structured summary for a health worker, checks it against a fixed list of danger signs, and routes it to a person. No automated part of it makes a clinical decision.
