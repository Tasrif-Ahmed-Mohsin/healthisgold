Privacy Policy

Healthcare Coordination Project · Last updated 23 September 2026

Current status: this is an academic project and is not a healthcare service. It is not open to the public, it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is currently operated with synthetic test data. If you have a medical concern, contact a registered doctor or your nearest health facility.

Who operates this

This project is operated by an individual student researcher in Bangladesh as academic work. It is not a registered company and does not currently hold a commercial relationship with any patient. Contact: tasrifahmedmohsin@gmail.com.

What information is processed

When the system is used, it may process:

  • Contact information — the phone number a message arrives from, and the display name the messaging provider reports.
  • Health information you provide — symptoms, duration, measurements such as temperature or blood pressure, and any medical history described in a message.
  • Media you send — voice notes, photographs of prescriptions or laboratory reports, and documents.
  • Message metadata — timestamps and message identifiers, used to avoid processing the same message twice.

Health information is sensitive personal data. It is only processed where you have given explicit consent, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Why it is processed

  • To organise what you have said into a structured summary a health worker can read.
  • To identify whether your situation needs urgent human attention.
  • To pass your case to a human health worker or clinician, with your information attached.
  • To keep a record of previous contacts so you do not have to repeat your history.

Automated processing, and its limits

Software is used to transcribe, translate and structure what you send, and to check it against a fixed list of danger signs. No automated system in this project makes a clinical decision about you. Automated processing can only raise how urgently a person reviews your case; it can never lower it, and it never replaces a clinician. Only a doctor registered with the Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council may diagnose or prescribe.

Who can see your information

  • Health workers and clinicians involved in coordinating your care.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, described below.

Your information is not sold, and is not used for advertising or marketing profiling.

Processing outside Bangladesh

Some processing uses third-party services whose servers may be outside Bangladesh — the messaging platform that delivers your message, and language-processing services used to transcribe and structure text. Where personal data is transferred outside Bangladesh, we do so only in line with the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026, and we will obtain the approvals that Act requires before processing real patient data.

How long it is kept

Case information is retained for as long as needed to coordinate your care and to maintain your medical history, and is deleted on request except where a record must be kept for a legal or clinical reason. Test and synthetic data is deleted when no longer needed for development.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026, you may:

  • ask what information about you is held;
  • ask for a copy of it;
  • ask for inaccurate information to be corrected;
  • ask for your information to be deleted;
  • withdraw your consent, which stops further processing.

To exercise any of these, email tasrifahmedmohsin@gmail.com, or see the data deletion instructions.

Security

Access to case information is limited to those coordinating care. Credentials are held in server configuration and never in client software. Message content is excluded from operational logs.

Children

Information about a child may be provided by a parent or guardian acting on their behalf, and is treated with the same protections described here.

Changes

This policy will be updated as the project develops. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

This document is a good-faith description of how the project handles information. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before this system processes information about any real patient, it will be reviewed against the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026, and a named registered clinician will hold clinical accountability.
